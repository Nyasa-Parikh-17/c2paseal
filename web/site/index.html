C2paSeal reads, verifies and signs C2PA Content Credentials in JPEG, PNG, WebP, HEIC and AVIF images. Drop a picture to check it. It runs in your browser, so the file never leaves your computer.

Check an image Try signing one Quickstart

No upload, no account. After the first visit the checker also works offline.

Check

Who signed it, when, what was done to it, and its edit history. Every C2PA status code is listed. Load the official C2PA trust list to see which signers are trusted.

Sign

Add Content Credentials to your own picture with a demo certificate, then download it and check it anywhere. Real signing uses your own certificate, through the tools below.

Recover

Credentials are often stripped when a picture is shared. If it carries a C2paSeal watermark, they can be found again in a manifest store and checked against the copy.

For developers

The checker is the open-source C2paSeal Go library compiled to WebAssembly. The same library signs in your own systems, with no CGO and no external tools, and its results are checked against c2patool, the C2PA reference tool.

What a check tells you

A valid result means the credentials are intact and match the picture, and says who signed them. It does not mean the picture shows what really happened: C2PA records what the signer claims. What C2PA does and does not prove.
